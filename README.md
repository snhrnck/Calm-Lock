# Calm Lock 🛡️🌿

**Calm Lock** is a lightweight, elegant Android application that automatically changes your lock screen wallpaper. 

Personalize your lock screen with your favorite photos and let Calm Lock take care of the rest!

## ✨ Features
*   **⏱️ Timer Mode** — Automatically changes your wallpaper at your chosen time intervals.
*   **🔒 Lock Mode** — Changes your wallpaper whenever your screen turns off, with built-in protection against excessive wallpaper changes.
*   **🔀 Sequential or Random** — Choose whether your photos appear in order or in a random sequence.
*   **🛡️ Privacy First** — No internet permission required, no analytics, and no data collection. Your photos stay securely stored on your device.
*   **🌍 12 Languages** — Fully localized interface with support for 12 languages.

## 📲 Installation
Visit the [Releases](../../releases) section. Download the latest APK version. Install it on your Android device. Open Calm Lock, select your favorite photos, and enjoy your personalized lock screen!
